Governance and protection

  • A one-page report by the 7th of every month: cash in, cash out, balance, milestone status, one metric per venture, and what slipped with the reason. Same shape every time, including quiet months.
  • Separate money. Each venture has its own account. Nothing personal is paid from it, and nothing venture-related is paid from personal funds without a recorded reimbursement.
  • Receipts above ₦50,000, filed and shared with the monthly report. Tranche money is spent on the lines it was released for.
  • Tranche gates. Money is released against measurable conditions, not dates. If a gate is missed, the next tranche waits and I report why.
  • Information rights. Monthly report, quarterly call, and a written answer to any question about spend within five working days. Annual accounts reviewed by an accountant once revenue passes ₦20m.
  • Protections. Pro-rata on future rounds, tag-along on any founder share sale, right of first refusal on transfers, and no related-party spending without written disclosure.
  • Reserved matters (from ₦14m): new borrowing above ₦5m, sale of material assets, a change in the nature of the business, or issuing shares below the last round price.
  • Buyback. After 24 months, either side may require the purchase of the position at 2× the amount invested, payable over six months from available cash. This is the answer to “how do I get out of a small company?”
