How the numbers were arrived at

The rates come from two mainland Lagos workshops and current published price guides, not from a round figure:

  • Painting: supply and apply, two coats, runs ₦1,200–₦2,500 per m² in Lagos in late 2026. The options sit at the lower-middle of that band, which is appropriate for a rental-standard finish with good paint.
  • Base cabinets: ₦95,000–₦170,000 per running metre at island showroom rates. Mainland workshops quote 10–25% below that for the same MDF specification, which is what these prices reflect.
  • Wall cabinets: ₦70,000–₦120,000 per running metre, same specification basis.
  • Wardrobe: a 1.8 m × 2.4 m MDF-laminate unit typically quotes at ₦180,000–₦280,000 supplied and installed. The figure here is at the bottom of that range.
  • Labour: furniture carpenters currently charge ₦15,000–₦22,000 a day in Lagos, and painters around ₦400–₦900 per m² labour only. Both are inside the line prices rather than billed separately.

Sources: published Nigerian cost guides for painting, screeding and carpentry rates, checked in November 2026. Rates move; the two-quote step exists so that the final price is confirmed by vendors rather than by this document.

What you will get back. Receipts for every purchase, photographs at each stage, a one-page closing report showing planned against actual spend, and whatever is left over returned with the report. If the total comes in under the option chosen, the difference comes back to you rather than being absorbed.
