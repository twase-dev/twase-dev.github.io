Post-Meeting Kit

The 48 hours after
the meeting.

Deals are lost in the week after the meeting, not in the meeting. This is the sequence to run once you leave the room, written out so you are not composing it at 11pm. Pick the variant that matches what he actually said, send it the same day, then follow the calendar.

The sequence at a glance

WhenWhatWhy it is there
Same eveningOne WhatsApp message, variant A, B or CHe has just given you his time. The thank-you is the message; everything else rides along with it.
+24 hoursEmail with the forwardable linkSo he can send the pack to whoever he consults without forwarding seven attachments.
+7 daysOne short check-in with a single new factOne new fact, not a reminder. A reminder reads as pressure; a fact reads as progress.
+30 daysThe first monthly report, whether or not he has decidedIt proves the reporting promise before he has paid for it. Nothing else in this pack is as persuasive.
+90 daysEither the pilot result or a clean closeAn open loop that never closes costs you more attention than a “no” does.

Same day · WhatsApp

Variant A · He committed to something

Good evening sir, thank you for today — and for the furnishing offer, which I have taken seriously enough to cost properly.

Everything we discussed is in one link so you do not have to hold it in your head: twase.dev/investor/falaye — the master proposal, the two venture documents, the furnishing breakdown, and the valuation page with the milestone ratchet we talked about.

I will send the term sheet for the position you indicated by   so you have something concrete to react to. If anything in the pack reads differently from what you expected, tell me and I will correct it rather than defend it.

Variant B · He wants to think about it

Good evening sir, thank you for the time today and for the straight questions — especially on valuation.

I have put the whole pack behind one link: twase.dev/investor/falaye. Document 06 is the one written for the question you asked: it shows what the ₦120m is made of, what revenue it has to produce to justify itself, and a ratchet that drops the price to ₦90m or ₦80m if I miss the milestones. I would rather be priced down for missing a gate than argue the number today.

No action needed from you this week. I will send one update in seven days, and the first monthly report at the end of the month whether or not you have decided — so you can see what the reporting actually looks like before you commit to it.

Variant C · Furnishing only

Good evening sir, thank you for today. I have sent the furnishing breakdown on its own as Document 04 — carpentry and painting, three priced options, nothing bundled into it.

To be explicit, as I said in the room: that is support, not investment. No equity, no repayment expected, and no reporting obligation attached to it. If you would rather it were an interest-free advance against studio revenue, I will write it that way instead — your preference, not mine.

The investment documents stay in the pack for whenever, or if ever, they are useful. There is no expiry on the conversation.

+7 days · The one-new-fact message

WhatsApp · short by design

Good afternoon sir — one update, no action needed.

  (one real thing that happened this week: an install number now measurable, the API contract deployed, a technician signed, a deposit received)

I will have the first monthly report to you by the fifth. If the answer on your side is no, that is genuinely fine — just tell me and I will stop sending.

+30 days · The report that does the selling

Send the one-page monthly report even if the answer is still open. It is the only claim in the pack that can be proved before he spends anything, and it converts the conversation from “do I believe this person” into “do I like this reporting”.

LineWhat goes in it
CashOpening balance, money in, money out, closing balance. One line each.
ShippedWhat actually went live this month. If nothing shipped, say nothing shipped.
SlippedWhat was meant to ship and did not, and the new date.
The three numbersInstalls, activation, paying users. Where a number is still unmeasured, the word “unmeasured” and the date it will exist.
AskOne thing you need, or the word “nothing”.

Objections, and the one-line answer

What he saysWhat you say
“There is no revenue. How can it be worth ₦120m?”“It cannot be defended on earnings, so it is not. It is defended on gates, a ratchet down to ₦80m, and a buyback at 2×. If those do not satisfy you, the revenue-share route prices the same money without a valuation at all.”
“Why should I fund two businesses?”“You should not have to. Each stands alone, and either can be declined without affecting the other. The studio is what pays my bills while both are built.”
“Why not just borrow the money?”“Because the furnishing support is not a loan and I have not asked for one. The investment is equity because the risk is real and should be shared, not stacked on me as debt I cannot service in month three.”
“What happens if you stop?”“Key-person risk is named in Document 01 with the mitigation: the code, the accounts and the domain are company assets from day one, the reporting is monthly, and the buyback exists precisely for the case where I am the problem.”
“Let me think about it.”“Of course. I will send one update in seven days and the first monthly report at the end of the month, and then I will leave it with you. Tell me if you would rather I stopped.”
“Can I start smaller?”“Yes. ₦3.5m is the WorldWorkHub entry ticket at the same price per percent, and ₦3m is the PhoneRep floor. Nothing about the terms changes at a smaller size.”

What not to do

  • ✕ Do not send the PDFs as seven separate attachments. One link, one email.
  • ✕ Do not send a message that only asks whether he has read it. Every follow-up carries a fact or does not go out.
  • ✕ Do not revise the valuation mid-conversation because he paused. The ratchet is the concession; the price is the price.
  • ✕ Do not add a new venture, a new number or a new promise after the meeting. It reads as improvisation.
  • ✕ Do not let the furnishing support become the whole relationship. If he says yes to that and no to the rest, thank him properly, deliver it, and keep the investment conversation for a later date.
The one thing to get right. Whatever he decides, he should come out of this feeling that he was dealt with straight — that the numbers were his to check, the risks were named by me before he found them, and a “no” cost him nothing. That reputation is worth more than any single ticket in this pack.
