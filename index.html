How It Works

From brief to live
in four steps.

01

Brief

We talk. I ask the uncomfortable questions — about your brand, your customers, your goals. 30 minutes. No design yet. Just listening.

02

Design

I develop the visual direction and you see it before a single line of code is written. Two rounds of refinement — nothing moves forward without your sign-off.

03

Build

Clean code. Fast pages. Mobile-first. You get progress updates — no black boxes, no surprises. You see it taking shape as it's built.

04

Launch

Site goes live. I hand you the keys, a short guide on managing your content, and I stay available for questions in the first two weeks.
